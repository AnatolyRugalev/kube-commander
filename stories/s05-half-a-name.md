## The situation

Someone messages you: "can you check the thing that handles payments? I think it's
called check-something."

You do not know what kind of object it is. You do not know its namespace. You have
half of its name and nothing else.

## Your goal

Find it, and end up looking at it.

Then do the same for a second one, where the half-remembered part is not the
beginning of the name — you remember it has "front" in it somewhere.
