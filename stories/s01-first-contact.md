# S01 — First contact

- Main story: no
- Fixture: `stories/cluster` — run `up.sh` first
- Trace: `~/traces/s01.jsonl`

## The situation

Somebody hands you a cluster you have never seen and asks what is running on it.
You have no idea what is deployed, in how many namespaces, or whether any of it is
healthy. You have kubecom and nothing else — no `kubectl`, no dashboard, no wiki
page.

## Your goal

Get oriented. By the time you stop you should be able to say, out loud, roughly
what this cluster is running and whether anything is wrong with it.

Take as long as it takes. Wander. If you find yourself wanting something that is
not there, that is the most useful thing that can happen in this story — keep
going anyway.

## When you're done

Tell me:

1. What you'd say the cluster is running, in a sentence.
2. Where you went first, and whether that turned out to be the right place.
3. Anything you expected to be one step away that turned out not to be.
