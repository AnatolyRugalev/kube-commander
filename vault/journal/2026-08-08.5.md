## Why a question flagged "only a human can answer" got answered here

The item's note said both questions were judgements a test cannot make, and that is why it
sat. It is worth writing down that the note was half wrong, because the same shape will
recur. Question 1 was phrased as *legibility* — "do the bar and the highlight stay legible
together" — and legibility is indeed an eye's verdict. But the thing that would make them
*illegible* together is a single number: how far apart the two backgrounds are. kubecom
already computes it (`styles.ContrastRatio`, `luminance.go`), for exactly this class of
question, on the whole registry, in a test. So the leg measured it:

| | matched text on its highlight | highlight vs the cursor bar |
|---|---|---|
| best | 13.81 catppuccin-mocha | 9.89 catppuccin-mocha |
| worst dark | 4.05 solarized-dark | 4.05 solarized-dark |
| catppuccin-latte | **2.15** | **1.70** |
| solarized-light | **2.62** | **2.62** |

The middle column across the eleven dark palettes is 4.05–9.89:1 — the two backgrounds are
comfortably distinguishable, which is the coexistence D242 pt 3 asserted and D239 argued for
the table. That is not the same as "a human likes it", so item 6 of the crashloop dogfood
now asks for the eye, with the numbers in hand and the two known-bad palettes ruled out. The
difference from before is that a dogfood that never happens no longer blocks the answer —
what the human can still overturn is narrow and named.

The general lesson: **a question is only a judgement below the level where the codebase has a
number.** Two legs treated "legibility" as atomic; it decomposes into a measurable part and a
taste part, and the measurable part was 90% of it.

## The defect the measurement found

`styles.New` builds `Match` as `Foreground(StatusBarBg)` over `Background(Warn)` — a dark
chrome shade on the palette's yellow. That is a sound reading of "highlighter pen" while
`StatusBarBg` is dark, and it was dark in all eleven built-ins until yesterday. THEME-04b
admitted `catppuccin-latte` and `solarized-light`, whose `StatusBarBg` is near-white, so the
role now renders **near-white text on mid yellow**: 2.15:1 and 2.62:1, against a 4.5:1 floor
every other text pair in those palettes clears. On Latte the highlight is also within 1.70:1
of the `Selection` bar, so under the cursor it nearly dissolves into it.

Two things make this worth its own slice rather than a fix appended here:

- **No existing role fixes it.** Every candidate colour in both palettes was measured against
  that palette's own `Warn`; the best are Latte `Foreground` at 3.05 and Solarized Light
  `StatusBarFg` at 4.05. Both under the floor, because these schemes' yellows sit
  mid-luminance and a light palette holds nothing dark enough above them. So the slice needs
  a *new* mechanism — a derived shade, or marking a match by weight where paint cannot carry
  it — and that is a decision with a diff, not a one-line swap.
- **It is not a theme-only bug.** `styles.Match` paints the logs grep, the table's `/` filter
  (FILT-02) and cluster-search hits (SEARCH-06). One style, three surfaces, one fix.

THEME-04b was right that its two ports were transcribed and measured; what neither it nor
this leg's predecessors measured is what the *rest of the styles* do once a light palette
exists. `Match` is the one that broke. The other unguarded roles (`Header`, `Subtle`,
`Primary`) were already surveyed by THEME-04b and left deliberately, and that reasoning still
holds — they are upstream's own values in roles that are not body text.

## Why no yank-all binding

`y` with no selection already copies the cursor's line, and `gg v G y` copies the buffer —
both live, and the README's logs section already teaches the second one verbatim, so the
composition is discoverable and not folklore. A `logs.yankAll` action would therefore add a
second name for a gesture that works, and it would have to answer a question the composition
does not raise: *what* is "everything visible"? With `w` on, a screen row is a fragment of a
log line; with a `/` query, the buffer and the shown set differ; with LOGS-07's 10 000-line
cap, "everything" is already a window. `gg v G y` has one meaning under all three — every
shown log line — because it is built out of the cursor, which counts log lines (D242 pt 2).
D252 pt 2 records the answer with the condition attached rather than a flat no: if a dogfood
does ask for it, it must be defined over shown log lines and inherit the cap.

## Scope: what this leg did not do

No code changed, which for a "decide the open questions" item is the intended shape — but it
means the illegible highlight is still shipping. It was left alone on purpose: fixing it well
needs the mechanism decision above, and bolting a light-palette special case onto this leg
would have produced exactly the per-palette exemption D251 pt 3 forbids. THEME-05 is the top
of the board's THEME line and the suggested next leg for that reason.
