# 2026-08-07 — LOGS-07: the logs buffer had no floor, only a rate

- Agent: claude-opus-5 (do-rewrite-leg)
- Milestone: none (feedback-driven LOGS line, closed by this)
- Did: bounded the logs view's buffer. `logsview.appendLine` appended to `lines`,
  `stamps` and `shownLines` and dropped nothing ever, so a view left following a chatty
  container (~1,900 lines/sec, D162's dogfood) grew all three for as long as it was open —
  the one thing neither `TailLines` (bounds the *replay*) nor LOGS-05b (bounds what a line
  *costs*) touched (D230). Now a `trim` runs after each append: past `MaxLines`+`trimChunk`
  (10 000 + 1 000) the oldest are deleted back to 10 000, and everything that addresses a
  line by index moves with them — `shownIdx` renumbered and its dropped-prefix entries
  evicted, the cursor and the selection anchor shifted by that eviction, the viewport's
  y-offset pulled down by the rows that left so a *paused* reader is not scrolled by the
  stream. The header gains `[trimmed]` once anything has gone, because otherwise `gg` lands
  mid-log in something that looks like the start of one.
- Decisions: **D245** — the cap must exceed the opening replay (a cap at or under
  `defaultLogTail` would discard, on the first live line, history the reader just fetched;
  the two constants are in different packages, so a test in `internal/tui` holds the
  inequality); the trim drops in chunks so it stays amortized O(1) per line, and *deletes*
  rather than reslices so the dropped strings are actually unreachable; a trim is the one
  event that moves all four index-space things at once; and `[trimmed]` is a fixed word,
  not a count.
- Files: `internal/tui/components/logsview/logsview.go` (`MaxLines`/`trimChunk`, `trimmed`,

  catch the y-offset arithmetic — every index-only assertion passes with the offset left
  alone, and the defect is a frame that slides under a reader who pressed pause.
