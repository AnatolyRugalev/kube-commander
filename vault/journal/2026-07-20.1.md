  `SetTable` installs a snapshot, recomputes visible columns + widths, resets
  selection to row 0 (M2-06b will apply deltas preserving selection). While
  rendering I hit and fixed a lipgloss gotcha: a bordered `Pane` counts its border
  *inside* `Style.Width`/`Height`, so the frame must be sized to the component's
  total w×h (content area = inner (w-2)×(h-2)); sizing it to the inner width wrapped
  every full-width row.
