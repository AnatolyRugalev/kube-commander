- Decisions: D184 — five constraints: the image ships the *released* artifact and never
  rebuilds it (the 2020 Dockerfile's `golang:` build stage shipped a different binary with no
  version ldflags, and nothing about that is visible from outside); `dockers_v2` over the
  deprecated `dockers:`+`docker_manifests:` pair, which makes the docker-container buildx
  driver load-bearing rather than boilerplate; distroless **root**, because a 0600 kubeconfig
  is unreadable to uid 65532 and `--user` leaves `$HOME` unwritable so kubecom won't start —
  and `scratch` is out because a missing CA bundle fails x509 only against a real cluster;
  Docker is the one publisher with nothing to make inert, so D173 pt 2's `skip_upload` pattern
  has no analogue and its absence is deliberate; and `latest` is `.Prerelease`-guarded, since
  Docker has no native pre-release notion and M5-10 recommends cutting an rc first.
