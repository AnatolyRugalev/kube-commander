- Three choices worth naming. (1) **Debounce, not per-keystroke**: every
  `QueryChangedMsg` cancels the in-flight fan-out and arms a 250 ms tick, and only a
  tick whose `searchGen` still matches launches a search — otherwise typing `nginx`
  would fire five concurrent sweeps of eleven kinds at a real cluster, which is the one
  way this feature could become a liability on a big cluster (D68). The in-flight
  indicator goes up on the keystroke, not on the launch, so the wait is never a silent
  blank. (2) **One staleness clock**: `searchGen` bumps on a query change and on close
  (never on mere completion) and tags both the debounce tick and every pumped hit, so a
  late hit from a cancelled fan-out is dropped *and* its pump chain stops rather than
  racing a second reader onto the live channel — the `watchGen`/`viewerGen` pattern.
  (3) **Drill-in switches kind now, selects later**: the object's row does not exist
  until the fresh watch's first RESET lands, so the hit's ref is stashed as a *pending
  selection* (`searchTarget`) that the watch pump applies via a new
  `table.SelectObject` (namespace+name match over the displayed rows) and clears on
  first hit, so later deltas never yank the reader back to it; `selectResource` drops
  the pending target so a menu switch can't inherit it.
- Scope is derived, not stored: the kind set comes from `menu.Items()` (the resource
  palette's source) filtered through `CommonSearchResources`, so discovered kinds and
  per-context extras are in, denied ones are out, and there is no second copy of the
  discovery result to keep in sync. An empty curated scope degrades to `no matches`
  rather than an indicator that never clears. `ctrl+s` had to be a no-text chord: the
  view's query field is always open (D140 pt 1), so any letter default would type
  instead of firing; app.quit's chord closes the view (help/viewer precedent) and `q`
  types a `q`.
- Decisions: D141 — search view is the body not an overlay; debounced launch with a
  generation-guarded tick + hit pump; `searchGen` is the single staleness clock;
  drill-in via a pending selection applied by the watch pump; scope derived from the
  menu's available kinds; `search.cluster` = `ctrl+s` (a chord by necessity).
