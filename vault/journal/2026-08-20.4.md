# 2026-08-20 — STORY-06h-1: the viewer fills the right pane

- Agent: claude-opus (do-rewrite-leg)
- Milestone: M5 (UX-validation line, D268)
- Did: Folded in `2026-08-15-describe-replaces-right-pane.md`, the S02 finding that the
  describe view "opens as a popup/overlay that does not take the full right pane — it
  wastes the space the pane would give it", with the walk's own evidence: describe was
  the on-call story's primary diagnostic (3 opens) and needed half-page-downs
  immediately. STORY-06h was **split on pickup** into this layout half and 06h-2 (the
  painted panel) — the panel needs the room before it can carry colour.
  Three edits. **viewer**: `screenMargin`/`minWidth`/`minHeight` are gone; `boxSize`
  returns exactly the area `SetSize` was given, so the box fills its pane edge to edge.
  The feedback's aside ("the same probably applies to the other shared-viewer surfaces")
  is answered for free — describe, YAML, secret and events all ride this one component.
  **overlay**: a new `overlayAt(base, box, x, y, w, h)` places a box at a given origin;
  `overlayCenter` is now the special case that computes the centering origin and calls
  it, so both keep one clamp/canvas contract.
  **shell**: `resize` sizes the viewer to the *table's* geometry (`paneWidths`, a new
  helper that both the pane sizing and the composite read), and `View` composites it at
  `rightPaneX()` instead of centering. On an 80×24 terminal the readable window goes
  from 74×15 to 58×19: **four more lines** of the dump and the pane's full height, and
  the width it gives up is the width the old inset was stealing from the menu it floated
  over — which is the trade the feedback asked for ("the viewer gets the whole
  right-pane width/height, not a modal inset").
  The composite exposed a layout quirk worth writing down: the menu's frame renders
  **two columns narrower** than the width `resize` sizes it to (its `View` hands
  `m.width-2` to a border-box frame, while the table hands its full width), so the panes
  meet at 18 on an 80-column terminal, not at `menuPaneWidth`'s 20. Composing at the
  computed boundary put the pager 2 columns off the pane it was replacing; reading the
  boundary off the rendered menu is what makes it exact. The 2-column dead gap that same
  quirk leaves at the body's right edge is real (`browseBody` is 78 wide on an 80-column
  terminal) and is filed as **BOX-04**, reopening the BOX section — out of this leg's
  scope, since fixing it means settling one border-box convention across both panes.
- Decisions: **D284** — a pager fills the pane, only popups overlay: the shared viewer is
  sized to and composited over the browse view's right pane (D95 unchanged for help /
  pickers / confirm / the forwards panel, the test being whether the surface's content
  *is* the thing being read), and anything composited onto the right pane takes its
  origin from `rightPaneX()`, never from `menuPaneWidth`.
