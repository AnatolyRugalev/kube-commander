# 2026-08-15 — STORY-06a: the letter remap — search ctrl+f, namespace N, delete D, describe d, plus the two displaced homes

- Agent: opencode (do-rewrite-leg, interactive session)
- Milestone: M5 (UX-validation line, D268)
- Did: Re-split STORY-06 into per-finding slices **06a…06m** on the board and landed the
  first, the letters half of the keymap redesign. The five walks' dead ends were
  concentrated in two key families — the n-family (namespace vs next/previous match) and
  the s-family (search vs sort vs sort-clear) — and the master redesign
  (`2026-08-15-keymap-redesign.md`) names the whole intended map. The **letters** moved:
  `search.cluster` `ctrl+s`→`ctrl+f` (browser convention, frees `ctrl+s`); `ns.switch`
  `ctrl+n`→`N` (joins the capital switcher family, frees `ctrl+n`); `res.delete` `d`→`D`
  and `res.describe` `D`→`d` (destructive on the capital); and the two displaced keys got
  new homes in the same change — `app.searchPrev` `N`→`#` (vim's backward-occurrence
  gesture, out of the n-family entirely) and `nav.pageDown` `ctrl+f`→`space` (vim's
  `<space>`-scrolls-a-screen, pairing the kept `ctrl+b` pageUp). `logs.select` gained `V`
  beside `v` — the S01 walk's linewise-visual reach. `navChords` (the Merge warning set)
  now tracks the navigation keys of the day (`#`/`space` in, `N`/`ctrl+f` out). The
  registry-generated artifacts rode along: `docs/keybindings.md` regenerated, the tape's
  describe/search steps and captions updated (`d`, `ctrl+f`), the default-resolution tests
  extended, and the tui test helpers (`describeKey`/`deleteKey`/`nsKey`/`searchKey`) and
  the numerous `ctrl+n` comments moved to the new keys. Deleted the master
  `keymap-redesign.md` plus the six items it supersedes — `context-switch-key-inconsistent`,
  `s-key-family-confusion`, `sort-column-picker`, `enter-actions-menu`,
  `log-line-selection-shift-v`, `namespace-switch-inconsistent` — the S-interaction and
  enter→menu designs now carried by the 06b/06c board notes. One real find on the way: the
  narrower keys tripped a bubbles/help boundary bug — its full layout adds a column whole
  when its ellipsis cannot fit, so cumulative column widths landing exactly on the limit
  dump *every* remaining column (the overlay went 102→676 cols wide). Fixed with a new
  `elide.Width` (the horizontal twin of `elide.Lines`, marking its own cut), applied to the
  help overlay before the existing height clamp.
- Decisions: **D269** — the letter remap and its rule: one verb one easy key, switchers are
  capitals, search is the browser key; a remap gives a displaced action its new home in the
  same change; `#` and `space` are the current navigation homes and must not slide back into
  the letter families.
