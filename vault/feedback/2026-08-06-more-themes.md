# Add more themes (~10 total), including Catppuccin

- Submitted: 2026-08-06
- Priority: normal
- Area: theming

We need more built-in themes — aim for around 10 total. Catppuccin is very
popular right now and should be one of them (it ships as multiple flavors —
Latte/Frappé/Macchiato/Mocha — pick which flavor(s) make sense as distinct
themes vs one). Research other popular terminal/TUI color schemes worth
including (e.g. things in the Dracula / Nord / Gruvbox / Solarized /
Tokyo Night / Rosé Pine family are the kind of names that come up a lot,
but don't just take my list — check what's actually popular).

Before implementing any of them: check licensing. Most of these palettes are
published under permissive licenses (MIT etc.) that allow reimplementing the
color values, but verify per-theme rather than assuming, and note the
license/attribution for each one added.
